Invoice vs PO vs receipt matching (3-way match)

Checks each supplier invoice against its purchase order and goods receipt before payment.

What it does

  • Holds an invoice when the price is outside tolerance (2%, adjustable), more units are billed than received, there is no PO, or it is a duplicate.
  • Writes a PAY / HOLD list with a reason on every line.
  • A person approves. The tool never pays anything.
