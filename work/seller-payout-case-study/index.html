Case study (sample): payout cleanup for an online seller

How a one-time catch-up check finds money that never reached the bank.

Sample records, not a real client. This case study uses made-up files to show the process and the kind of result to expect.

The situation

A small seller on Shopify and Amazon had not matched payouts to the bank for months. Fees, refunds and chargebacks made the totals hard to follow, and nobody knew if every payout had arrived.

What was done

  1. Collected the Shopify payouts export, the Amazon settlement report and the bank statement.
  2. Matched each payout to a bank deposit (exact amount, then small tolerance, then partial), using each deposit only once.
  3. Checked fees against estimated rates, and checked refunds, chargebacks and refund commissions.
  4. Wrote a short report with each finding and what to do next, then checked it against a QA list.
