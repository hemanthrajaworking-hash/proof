AI answer testing & safety checks

An offline lab that checks whether a chatbot or AI answer is correct, grounded in the documents, and safe.

What it does

  • Scores answers with weighted rubrics and an optional local AI judge.
  • Flags made-up numbers and forbidden claims.
  • Measures document search quality (recall@k, MRR, nDCG).
  • Red-team tests: prompt injection, invoice fraud, privacy, false premise, impersonation. An item passes only if the bot refuses and leaks nothing.
  • A release gate fails automatically when quality drops. In the demo it caught recall falling from 1.0 to 0.875 when search depth was cut.
