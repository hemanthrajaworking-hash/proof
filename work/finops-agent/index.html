Month-end close pipeline (8 steps, human approval)

A multi-step finance workflow: invoice intake, 3-way match, bank match, tax credit match, variance report, report checker and an approval gate.

What it does

  • A planner runs 8 steps in the right order from their inputs and outputs.
  • A checker scores the written report and blocks over-claims such as "no exceptions". The report must score at least 0.80 to pass.
  • Nothing is paid or claimed until a named person approves.
  • Every step is written to a tamper-evident (hash-chained) audit log, with a web dashboard per run.
  • Works with a free local model, and falls back to a fixed-template report if the model is slow.
