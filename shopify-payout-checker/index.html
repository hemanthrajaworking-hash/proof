    How it matches

    1. Exact amount, deposit dated on or up to N days after the payout date (closest date wins, each deposit used once, so two payouts of the same amount are not double-counted).
    2. Within the amount tolerance (rounding or a small bank charge).
    3. Partial: same window, but the deposit is short or over by more than the tolerance.
    4. Anything left is a missing deposit, or a Shopify-labelled deposit with no payout row.

    Limits: it does not combine several payouts into one deposit, does not convert currencies, and cannot see orders behind a payout. It is a quick check, not an audit.
